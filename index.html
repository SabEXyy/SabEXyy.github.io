CallBluff - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 27, 2026

Developer: KnowerApps (Shivam Prajapati)

Overview

CallBluff is a fake call simulation app. It does NOT make real phone calls, access your contacts, or record any data.

Data Collection

CallBluff does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All data (contacts, ringtone preferences) is stored locally on your device only.

Permissions

  • Notifications: To show incoming call notifications
  • Display over other apps: To show the call screen when the app is in the background
  • Vibrate: For call vibration effect
  • Wake Lock: To wake the screen for incoming calls

These permissions are used solely for simulating fake calls and are never used to access personal data.

Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad personalization. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob (advertising)

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
